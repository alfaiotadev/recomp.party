# Platform logos

Logos are trademarks of their respective owners and are used only to identify the platforms.
The files themselves are public domain (simple geometric/text logos, per Wikimedia Commons) or CC0 (Simple Icons).
They were sanitised (no scripts, no external references), background-stripped where needed and cropped to content.

`snes-mark.svg` is `snes.svg` with the viewBox cropped to the "SUPER NINTENDO" wordmark: the
"ENTERTAINMENT SYSTEM" bar is unreadable at card size.

`nes.svg` drops the white fill inside the "Nintendo" oval and `gba.svg` drops the blue badge behind the
letters: the site renders logos as white silhouettes, and those fills would merge the lettering into a blob.
